FAQ

How long does sun keep my file?

sun keeps your file for 7 days. After 7 days, sun deletes the file. sun does not warn you before deletion. Save a copy if you need the file for longer.

What are the upload limits?

A single file cannot be larger than 2GB. Per IP address, sun also applies three rolling limits:

  • 2GB per hour
  • 5GB per day
  • 10GB per week

If you go over a limit, sun rejects the upload with an error. Wait for the limit to reset, then try again.

What data does sun collect?

sun records your IP address for each upload. sun uses this to enforce the upload limits above.

sun does not require an account. sun does not collect your name or email. sun does not link your IP address to a profile.

How do I report abuse?

To report a file, contact me@arda0.net.
